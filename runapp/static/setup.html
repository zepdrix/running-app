2Connect Strava

Strava only lets an app you register yourself read your account. It's free and takes about two minutes:

  1. Open strava.com/settings/api and sign in.
  2. Create an app. Any name and website will do (for the website, http://localhost works). For Authorization Callback Domain enter localhost
  3. Copy the Client ID and the Client Secret from that page into the boxes below.

Strava then asks you to approve access. Leave “View data about your private activities” ticked so all your runs come through. The keys are saved on this computer only.
